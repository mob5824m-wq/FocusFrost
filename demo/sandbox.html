A 60-second tour

  1. Open a tracked site. Pick youtube.com and hit Open tab. The frosty chip appears bottom-right with the grace countdown.
  2. Try the grace window. Hit Leave tab then Return quickly — you come back for free, same visit.
  3. Wait out the grace. Use +1 min to age the page, then Leave tab. The tab is closed at once and the site starts cooling down.
  4. Hit the wall. Try to open it again: you land on the cooling-down page with the real countdown.
  5. Need it for real? In the popup, pick a length and hold the button for six seconds. Two per day, 30 minutes apart.

The sandbox drives time with buttons instead of waiting, but every decision comes from the same engine.js the installed extension runs.
