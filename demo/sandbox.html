A 60-second tour

  1. Open a tracked site. Pick youtube.com and hit Open tab. You land on the intervention screen, not the site — that is the one-sec half of the design.
  2. Get through the gate. Sit out the breath (4 seconds here), read the "do this instead" line, then either close the tab or continue with an intention. Only then does the frosty chip appear with the grace countdown.
  3. Try the grace window. Hit Leave tab then Return quickly — you come back for free, same visit. Wait past it and leave, and the tab closes and the site starts cooling down.
  4. Hit the wall. Try to open it again: you land on the cooling-down page with the real countdown. The gate does not rescue you from a cooldown.
  5. Need it for real? On the cooling-down page, pick a length and hold the pause button for six seconds. Two per day, 30 minutes apart.
  6. Change anything. Settings page → opens the real full-page settings app: interventions, focus hours, pause rationing, intentions.

The sandbox drives time with buttons instead of waiting, but every decision comes from the same engine.js the installed extension runs.
