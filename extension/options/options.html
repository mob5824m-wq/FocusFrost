How the clock actually works

  1. Grace. The clock starts the second the page opens. Leave and come back inside the grace window and nothing happens — no penalty, no reset.
  2. Focus. Past grace, the stopwatch only runs while the tab is visible and the window has focus. Stay put and you get the full focus cap.
  3. Cooldown. Leave, close, or hit the cap and the site locks for its cooldown. Reloading, restarting the browser, or opening a new tab all hit the same wall.
